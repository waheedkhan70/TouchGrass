Open Source Core

The Problem with Proprietary Cloud AI in the Wild

When you hike deep into a national forest, river valley, or mountain ravine to touch grass, cell signal disappears. Proprietary APIs fail, leaving you stranded with spinning loaders. Worse, your personal GPS tracks and audio recordings get shipped off to distant server farms.

Why TouchGrass AI is Built on Open Weights

📡
100% Offline Trail Independence: Runs locally in the browser via ONNX Runtime / WebAssembly. No network packets leave your device while you are in the woods.
🔒
Total Trailhead Privacy: Your bio-acoustic recordings and run club locations stay in your local browser storage. No surveillance capitalism in the forest.
💵
$0.00 Infinite Execution: Zero token fees, zero monthly subscriptions. You can listen to forest calls all morning without fear of API billing limits.
🦙
Pluggable Local LLMs (Ollama / Llama 3.2): Have a laptop or phone running Ollama? Connect directly to localhost:11434 with one click for rich naturalist field notes with open weights.
